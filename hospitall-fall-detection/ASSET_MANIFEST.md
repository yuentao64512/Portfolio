# Hospital project asset manifest

This folder is a GitHub-safe snapshot of `/home/robotx/fall_action_project` on the RTX 5060 workstation.

The local Python virtual environment, raw/private datasets, raw DJI recordings, cache files, logs, and large generated training runs are intentionally excluded. They remain on the 5060 and can be regenerated or restored from their original sources.

Committed outputs are lightweight evidence and reproducibility artifacts; they are not a substitute for independent live-camera validation.
